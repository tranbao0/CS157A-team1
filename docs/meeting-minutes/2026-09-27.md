### Decisions

- **Planner, not scheduler.** The MeetsOn relationship and most section scheduling details were removed.
  Sections stay only to count open seats and to tell lecture sections from lab sections.
  All three agreed not to add a scheduler for now.
- **16 tables:** 12 entity sets plus 4 many-to-many relationships.
- **PlannedSession** connects PlanEntries to Sessions, not to Terms (raised by Michael).
- **EnrollmentSnapshots** became a weak entity set, with ObservedAs as its identifying relationship.
- **RequiresGE stays many-to-many:** a roadmap requires many GE areas, and one GE area is required by many roadmaps (question from Travis, answered by Bao).
- The full database script was shared in Discord for everyone to build the tables locally.
